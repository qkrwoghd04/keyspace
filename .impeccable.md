# Design Context

- Audience: the creator is a frontend developer building a personal website; visitors should immediately enjoy typing and playing with a crafted object.
- Primary use: type with a physical keyboard, see independent 3D key presses in real time, edit native multilingual text, and tap virtual keys. The experience fits one desktop viewport and adapts to mobile.
- Personality: restrained, precise, tactile, warm, playful through physical feedback. A premium custom mechanical keyboard photographed in a studio.
- Direction: warm off-white background, charcoal matte aluminum case, ivory and warm-gray sculpted keys, one muted orange Escape key. Soft shadows, highly legible legends, understated serif editable text with a compact sans-serif wordmark and controls.
- Composition: one keyboard is the protagonist. Small KEYSPACE wordmark at the top, native text area above the object, minimal Reset and Sound controls below. No large marketing introduction, projects, technology lists, decorative particles, or gaming RGB.
- Behavior: immediate press and measured spring return, restrained orange afterglow, optional muted-by-default sound, subtle parallax only when not typing. Respect reduced motion, focus, browser shortcuts, IME, and privacy.
